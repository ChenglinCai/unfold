# Example: supply and demand

This example holds hand-written files for one economics section, made during M1 to test the formats against a textbook. It has a source manifest, a knowledge map, an outline, and one segment's script and storyboard. Nothing here is rendered yet.

The source is section 3.1 of Principles of Economics 2e by Steven A. Greenlaw and David Shapiro, published by OpenStax in 2017 under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Access it for free at https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/1-introduction. These files adapt that section. The numbers come from its Tables 3.1 to 3.3.
