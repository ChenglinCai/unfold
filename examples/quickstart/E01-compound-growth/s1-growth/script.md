[[title]] Put one hundred dollars in a savings account that pays five percent a year.

[[growth]] After one year, you have one hundred five dollars. After two years, you have about one hundred ten dollars, because the interest earns interest too.

[[timeline]] Each year adds a little more than the year before. That small extra is compound growth.
