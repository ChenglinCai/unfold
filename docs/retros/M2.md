# Retro: M2, source understanding

M2 turned one source from each family into a source document, a knowledge map, and study notes. Six golden sources passed: a private lecture, MIT slides, two Wikipedia articles, a spoken Wikipedia recording, and a bare topic. Five passed their checks on the first try, and one needed a second.

## What was hard

1. **A bot wall.** The CUNY deck sits behind Cloudflare, which refuses downloads outside a browser. Getting around an access control is not our call, so MIT 18.05 slides took its place.
2. **Formulas vanished.** trafilatura drops every `<math>` element, so the Euler's identity article lost all its equations. Only reading the output showed it. The web reader now keeps each formula's TeX.
3. **Silence moved timestamps.** Speech after a long pause joined the segment before it and took its start time. A test with an 8-second silence caught it, and the voice filter fixed it.
4. **A menu counted as an article.** trafilatura returned "Home" from a page with only a menu. A page now needs 20 words of main text.
5. **Two libraries disagreed.** faster-whisper 1.2.1 passes an option that PyAV 19 removed, so unfold now decodes audio itself.
6. **A job could join this session.** A nested `claude` inherited this session's connection variables. The runner drops them, and a Haiku job confirmed that a job sees no tools.
7. **A hidden commit failure.** Pre-commit's pyright failed on a partial commit, and a pipe hid the failure. The mistake log has the guardrail.
8. **The model ignores the word limit.** The prompt asks for at most 25 words per sentence, yet 32 sentences in 3,966 words of notes run longer.
9. **The constitution and the spec disagree.** The spec asked for one job that writes two files. Principle II says a job writes one file, so the question waits for the maintainer.

## Numbers

- Ingest took under a second for each text source, and 12 seconds for 3.4 minutes of audio.
- Understand made 8 model calls for six sources, against a budget of 15. A later run made none.
- The calls used 65,508 input tokens and 55,973 output tokens, in about 9 minutes. Most output tokens were the model's thinking.
- All 154 anchor references and 193 citations resolve.
- The study notes hold 34 long sentences in 4,903 words.
- All 172 tests pass, including the 3 slow source tests.
